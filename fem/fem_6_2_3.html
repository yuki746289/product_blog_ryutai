・内挿関数N2の微分

4面体1次要素 の内挿関数N2の微分値を示します。



x微分

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{\partial N_2}{\partial x} &= \frac{\partial L_2}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial(V_2/V)}{\partial x} = \frac{1}{V}\frac{\partial V_2}{\partial x} \\ &= \frac{1}{V}\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\frac{1}{6} \Big( x y_3z_4-x z_3y_4-y x_3z_4+y z_3x_4+z x_3y_4-z y_3x_4 -x_1y_3z_4+x_1z_3y_4+x_1y z_4-x_1y z_3-x_1z y_4+x_1z y_3 +y_1x_3z_4-y_1z_3x_4-y_1x z_4+y_1x z_3+y_1z x_4-y_1z x_3 -z_1x_3y_4+z_1y_3x_4+z_1x y_4-z_1x y_3-z_1y x_4+z_1y x_3 \Big) \\ &= \frac{1}{6V}\Big( y_3z_4-z_3y_4-y_1z_4+y_1z_3+z_1y_4-z_1y_3 \Big) \end{aligned} \]


y微分

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{\partial N_2}{\partial y} &= \frac{\partial L_2}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial(V_2/V)}{\partial y} = \frac{1}{V}\frac{\partial V_2}{\partial y} \\ &= \frac{1}{V}\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\frac{1}{6} \Big( x y_3z_4-x z_3y_4-y x_3z_4+y z_3x_4+z x_3y_4-z y_3x_4 -x_1y_3z_4+x_1z_3y_4+x_1y z_4-x_1y z_3-x_1z y_4+x_1z y_3 +y_1x_3z_4-y_1z_3x_4-y_1x z_4+y_1x z_3+y_1z x_4-y_1z x_3 -z_1x_3y_4+z_1y_3x_4+z_1x y_4-z_1x y_3-z_1y x_4+z_1y x_3 \Big) \\ &= \frac{1}{6V}\Big( -x_3z_4+z_3x_4+x_1z_4-x_1z_3-z_1x_4+z_1x_3 \Big) \end{aligned} \]


z微分

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{\partial N_2}{\partial z} &= \frac{\partial L_2}{\partial z} = \frac{\partial(V_2/V)}{\partial z} = \frac{1}{V}\frac{\partial V_2}{\partial z} \\ &= \frac{1}{V}\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\frac{1}{6} \Big( x y_3z_4-x z_3y_4-y x_3z_4+y z_3x_4+z x_3y_4-z y_3x_4 -x_1y_3z_4+x_1z_3y_4+x_1y z_4-x_1y z_3-x_1z y_4+x_1z y_3 +y_1x_3z_4-y_1z_3x_4-y_1x z_4+y_1x z_3+y_1z x_4-y_1z x_3 -z_1x_3y_4+z_1y_3x_4+z_1x y_4-z_1x y_3-z_1y x_4+z_1y x_3 \Big) \\ &= \frac{1}{6V}\Big( x_3y_4-y_3x_4-x_1y_4+x_1y_3+y_1x_4-y_1x_3 \Big) \end{aligned} \]
