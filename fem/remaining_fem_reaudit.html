fem_4-002 (rich)

\[ \det A=\sum_{\sigma\in\sigma_n}\varepsilon(\sigma)a_{1\sigma(1)}a_{2\sigma(2)}\cdots a_{n\sigma(n)} \]

\(\varepsilon(\sigma)\)を \(\sigma\in\sigma_n\) の符号とすると

(1) \(\varepsilon(\tau\sigma)=\varepsilon(\sigma)\varepsilon(\tau)\)

(2) \(\varepsilon(1)=1\)

(3) \(\varepsilon(\sigma^{-1})=\varepsilon(\sigma)\)

(4) \(\rho\) が互換であれば \(\varepsilon(\rho)=-1\)
